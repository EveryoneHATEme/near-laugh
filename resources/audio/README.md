# P04 temporary audio

The five mono PCM16 WAVE clips are 48 kHz and less than 120 seconds each.
They are original fixture content generated by
[`prepare_audio_fixture.py`](../../scripts/prepare_audio_fixture.py), using
deterministic synthetic effects and eSpeak NG 1.52 formant speech. They are
covered by the repository's [GPLv3 license](../../LICENSE). No third-party
recorded performances, voice samples or music are included.

eSpeak NG is a separate GPLv3 preparation tool, not a game dependency. Its
[1.52 release and source](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng/releases/tag/1.52.0)
provide the tool license and regeneration source. The Windows release MSI used
for preparation has SHA-256
`7f673c709ea5dd579d3b5ebb98688cc575328a6ab7438d2bc405b88cedaeafb9`.
Only extracted command-line files were used; the tool is not packaged with
the game. Regeneration also requires ffmpeg for conversion to the fixed profile:

```sh
python scripts/prepare_audio_fixture.py --espeak /path/to/espeak-ng
```

The script's Russian text is the single source for speech synthesis and timed
captions. `fixture.sources.json` records output hashes and exact clip durations.
Timing includes a short lead-in and trailing silence for each spoken line.
Caption files are UTF-8: one segment per line, with start seconds, end seconds,
source label and text separated by tabs. Tabs and newlines cannot occur inside
labels or text. Times are relative to the matching catalog clip; each segment
must last at least one second and include at most 160 Unicode scalar values.

The radio uses an original five-note melody with quiet noise; the phone rings;
the footstep clip has ten spaced impacts. The phone conversation asks when
the caller will return, states that the caller is away until tomorrow, then
ends with a hang-up click. The later invitation claims the caller is already
home and asks Lena to open the door. The label only says “Голос за дверью”.

These synthetic voices are temporary acceptance content. Human listening for
Russian intelligibility, spatial placement, and measured device latency is
still required; generation and hashes alone do not establish those results.

## Neutral scripted-character effects

`python -B scripts/prepare_scripted_character_audio.py` reproducibly prepares
original seeded effects without speech tools or external source audio.
`scripted-characters-provenance.json` records the synthesis recipe, seed and
SHA-256 hashes. The mono PCM16/48 kHz footstep lasts 0.12 seconds and has no
caption. The interaction contains 0.20 seconds of effect followed by exact
silence through 1.00 seconds, with a matching one-second Russian caption.
The full second controls foreground arbitration and logical completion.
Runtime preflight verifies the selected duration, trailing silence and caption
bounds. Actor source links are exclusive, spatial, non-autoplay one-shots;
accepted travel triggers contacts and the final clip uses its calibrated marker.
